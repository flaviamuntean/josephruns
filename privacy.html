Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data I collect when you use this website or work with me as a running coach, why I collect it, and your rights over it.

Who I am

I’m Joseph Muntean-Lee, a running coach based in Farnham, Surrey, trading as Joseph Run Coach. I’m the data controller for the personal data described here. You can contact me about anything in this policy at josephmunteanlee@gmail.com.

What I collect

When you send an enquiry: your name, email address, running goal and anything you write in your message.

When you book an intro call: the details you enter on the booking form, usually your name, email address and chosen time.

When you buy a plan or coaching: your name, email address and billing details. Payments are handled by Stripe. I never see or store your full card details.

When I coach you: information about your running, such as training history, race goals, session data from your running app or watch (for example pace, distance and heart rate), and any injuries or health issues you choose to tell me about.

Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange an intro call.
  • To build your training plan and coach you.
  • To take payment and keep financial records I’m legally required to keep.

I use your data because it’s needed to answer your request or provide the service you’ve bought, and to meet legal obligations such as tax record-keeping. Information about injuries or health is more sensitive, so I only use it with your consent, to make your training safe and suitable. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

I don’t sell your data, use it for advertising or send marketing emails.

Who I share it with

I only share your data with the services that help me run the coaching:

  • Netlify, which hosts this website and receives enquiry form messages.
  • Cal.com, which handles intro call bookings.
  • Stripe, which processes payments and subscriptions.
  • Your running app (for example Strava or Garmin Connect), if you choose to share your activity data with me through it.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the fonts on this site. Your browser connects to Google to load them, which shares your IP address with Google.

Some of these services store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

Cookies

This website doesn’t use analytics or advertising cookies. The booking calendar and Stripe checkout may set cookies they need to work when you use them.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to coaching: up to 12 months.
  • Training and health information: while I’m coaching you and for up to 12 months afterwards, in case you come back.
  • Payment records: 6 years, as required by HMRC.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how I use it, and to receive it in a portable format. To do any of these, email me at josephmunteanlee@gmail.com and I’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. I’d appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes

If I change this policy, I’ll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
