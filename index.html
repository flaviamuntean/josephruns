FAQ

Do I need to be fast?

Not at all. Plans start from where you are now, whatever your pace.

What if I get injured?

On Coached and 1:1 Custom, we adjust the plan straight away and work around it, alongside advice from your physio. On Plan, pause your training and see a physio, then upgrade to Coached any time if you’d like an adjusted plan.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. You keep access until the end of the month you’ve paid for.

Which app do I need?

Strava. The free version is fine, and it syncs with most running watches.
